PlainScan turns a paper page into a PDF on your phone. Open it, tap Scan, and the camera is already up: there is no account to create and no watermark on the file you save.

Another guide: [Copy Text From a Paper Document on Android](/2026/11/09/copy-text-from-a-paper-document-on-android.html).

It is the right tool for receipts, invoices, contracts, forms, school notes, letters, and IDs. The walkthrough is [how to scan paper documents to a PDF on Android, offline](/2026/11/08/scan-documents-to-pdf-offline-android.html).

## What it does

- Finds the page edges and straightens the photo.
- Cleans shadows and stains, with optional filters.
- Puts many pages in one document, and lets you add pages later.
- Imports a photo that is already in the gallery.
- Reorders, rotates, or deletes pages.
- Shares or saves a PDF, shares images, or prints.
- Copies the text from a scan. The listing covers English, other Latin-alphabet languages, and Chinese. Recognition runs on the phone.
- Searches your documents by title or by the words inside them.
- Offers a light theme and a dark theme.

Two shortcuts are on the listing: long-press the icon for a "New scan" action, and an optional setting that opens the camera as soon as the app launches.

Scanning uses the on-device document scanner in Google Play services.

## Privacy

PlainScan has no internet permission. A document leaves the phone only when you share, save, or print it yourself. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account.

## Questions people ask

**Can I search a stack of receipts later?**
Yes. PlainScan keeps the text it reads, and search looks at titles and at the words inside the scans.

**Does the PDF get a watermark?**
The Play listing says there is no watermark, and no video you have to watch before saving.

**Which languages can it read?**
The listing names English, other Latin-alphabet languages, and Chinese.
