If one bedroom always drops the video call, your phone can show you where the signal falls off. [PlainWiFi](/apps/plainwifi/) records a walk through your home and ranks the dead zones, weakest first. It reads what Android reports and explains it in plain sentences. It does not fix the signal. That part is still up to you and your router.

These steps match the Play listing as of November 2026.

## Before you start

1. Install [PlainWiFi](/apps/plainwifi/). It opens on your networks.
2. Android only shows Wi-Fi scan results to apps that have location access. PlainWiFi explains why before it asks. It does not read or store your position. If you only want the live signal meter, that works with no permission at all.
3. Connect to the network you want to check. The meter follows the network you are on.

## Walk the rooms

1. Start a room walk in the app.
2. Take the phone into each room you care about and record it there.
3. The free walk measures 3 rooms, so choose them with care. A good set is the room with the problem, the room with the router as a baseline, and one room in between.
4. When you finish, PlainWiFi ranks the rooms from weakest to strongest. The top of that list is where to focus.

## Check a weak spot up close

A room can be fine by the door and bad in the corner where the desk is. The live signal meter shows this better than a single reading.

1. Open the signal meter for the network you are on.
2. Stand where you actually use the phone or laptop. Hold the phone at about that height.
3. Move slowly around the room. The meter updates every second and keeps a 60-second graph, so you can see where the line drops.
4. Read the quality label. It explains what the number means, so you do not have to guess whether a reading is good or bad.

If the scan list stops refreshing, that is Android limiting how often apps can scan. PlainWiFi keeps the last scan on screen and shows how old it is, so you are not looking at a blank list.

## What to do with the result

- If the weak room is just far from the router, moving the router somewhere more central is the usual first thing to try.
- If nearby networks are crowding yours, PlainWiFi also recommends the least crowded channel. The steps are in [how to find the least crowded Wi-Fi channel](/2026/11/08/find-the-least-crowded-wifi-channel.html). You change the channel in your router's own settings.
- To compare before and after a change, you need the optional one-time purchase. It adds saved walks, unlimited rooms per walk, and CSV export of scans and walks. There is no subscription. The Play listing does not publish a price.

## When it is the wrong tool

PlainWiFi does not run a speed test. If your question is "how fast is the internet in the bedroom", this app will not answer it. A strong signal and a fast connection are not the same thing. It also does not show saved Wi-Fi passwords, and it does not boost a network. It tells you where the signal is weak. It does not make it stronger.

The app has no internet permission. Nothing it sees leaves the phone unless you export it. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account.
