You can scan a paper page to a PDF on an Android phone without uploading it. [PlainScan](/apps/plainscan/) opens straight into the camera, finds the edges of the page, and saves a PDF that stays on the phone until you share it.

This is the flow I use for a receipt, a signed form, or a letter I want to keep and search later. It matches the Play listing as of November 2026.

## Scan one page

1. Install [PlainScan](/apps/plainscan/) from Google Play. There is no account to create.
2. Open the app and tap Scan. If you do this often, long-press the icon and use the "New scan" shortcut, or turn on the setting that opens the camera at launch.
3. Hold the phone over the page. The scanner finds the edges and straightens the photo. That scanner is the on-device document scanner in Google Play services.
4. If the page has a shadow or a stain, try the optional filters.
5. Save. Share the PDF, share the images, or print. The listing says the saved file has no watermark.

A page you already photographed can be imported from the gallery instead of shot again.

## Add the rest of the document

A contract or a set of notes is rarely one page. Scan the next page into the same document, or add it later. Reorder, rotate, or delete pages before you share.

Name the document something you will remember ("2026-11 rent receipt", "school form"). Search looks at the title and at the words inside the scans.

## Copy the text

PlainScan can copy the text from a scan. The listing covers English, other Latin-alphabet languages, and Chinese. Recognition runs on the phone. I use that to grab an invoice number or an address instead of retyping it.

## What stays on the phone

PlainScan has no internet permission. A document leaves the phone only when you share, save, or print it yourself. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account.

If the file is already a PDF on a computer and you need to fill or sign it, that is a different job. [FairFill](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/fairfill/) does it in Chrome. PlainScan is for paper that is not a PDF yet.
