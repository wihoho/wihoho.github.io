You can get the words off a printed page and into an email or a note without typing them again. [PlainScan](/apps/plainscan/) scans the page with your camera and copies the text from the scan. The text is read on the phone, so the page never has to be sent anywhere first.

These steps follow the Play listing as of November 2026. You don't need an account, and nothing you save gets a watermark.

## Scan the page

1. Install [PlainScan](/apps/plainscan/) and open it.
2. Tap Scan. The camera opens right away.
3. Hold the phone over the page. PlainScan finds the edges and straightens the photo, so you don't have to line it up exactly.
4. Let it clean up shadows and stains. There are optional filters too. A clean, even image is easier to read, so this step is worth a moment.
5. If a letter or contract runs over several pages, scan them all into the same document. You can add more pages later.

If the page is already a photo in your gallery, import that photo instead of scanning again.

## Copy the text

1. Open the scan.
2. Copy the text from it.
3. Paste it where you need it: an email, a note, a form field, a chat.

Read the pasted text once before you send it. I would check every number against the paper, because one wrong digit in an invoice or account number can cause real trouble.

## Which languages it reads

The listing names English, other Latin-alphabet languages, and Chinese. If your page is in one of those, PlainScan can read it.

## When it is the wrong tool

If the page uses a script the listing doesn't name, such as Arabic or Cyrillic, don't count on PlainScan to get the words out. You can still scan the page and save it as a PDF, but copying its text is not what the app promises.

## Find the text again later

PlainScan keeps the text it reads. Search checks document titles and the words inside the scans. So a pile of receipts, letters, or school notes can be searched by a shop name or a phrase you remember, even if you never gave the scan a useful title.

You can also share or save a PDF, share the pages as images, or print. Pages can be reordered, rotated, or deleted before you do.

## Two shortcuts if you do this often

- Long-press the app icon and pick "New scan".
- Turn on the setting that opens the camera as soon as the app starts.

## What stays on the phone

PlainScan has no internet permission. Scanning uses the on-device document scanner in Google Play services, and text recognition also runs on the phone. A document leaves the phone only when you share, save, or print it yourself. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no account.
