* [Android apps](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/) (2026): small, free tools on Google Play. Most have no account and no internet permission
    * [PlainScan](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainscan/): scan documents to PDF, offline, with on-device text
    * [PlainThread](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainthread/): turn a WhatsApp export or SMS backup into a dated PDF
    * [PlainWiFi](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainwifi/): see crowded Wi-Fi channels and dead zones
    * [PlainLoud](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainloud/): make a quiet video louder, without a watermark
    * [PlainPlan](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainplan/): draw floor plans to scale and export PDF or DXF
    * [PlainStamp](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainstamp/): stamp date, time, and GPS on photos and video
    * [PlainMark](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainmark/): watermark photos and videos at full size
    * [PlainPads](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plainpads/): drum pads, a step sequencer, and a looper
    * [PlainTally](https://wihoho.github.io/apps/plaintally/): scan barcodes and QR codes into CSV or XLSX

* [Chrome extensions](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/) (2025–2026): small, free tools that each do one job well, with no accounts
    * [DraftLens](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/draftlens/): open DWG and DXF drawings in Chrome, free, nothing uploaded
    * [SuggestScope](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/suggestscope/): free Amazon keyword research for sellers and KDP authors
    * [FairFill](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/fairfill/): fill and sign any PDF, no watermark
    * [NanoEvent](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/nanoevent/): turn emails, flyers and screenshots into calendar events
    * [OmniSearch](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/omnisearch/): search any site from the address bar
    * Also [ConvertLocal](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/convertlocal/), [SpeakType](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/speaktype/), [ClearRec](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/clearrec/) and [FocusRead](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/focusread/)
