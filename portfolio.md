* [Chrome extensions](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/) (2025–2026): small, free tools that each do one job well, with no accounts
    * [DraftLens](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/draftlens/): open DWG and DXF drawings in Chrome, free, nothing uploaded
    * [SuggestScope](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/suggestscope/): free Amazon keyword research for sellers and KDP authors
    * [FairFill](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/fairfill/): fill and sign any PDF, no watermark
    * [NanoEvent](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/nanoevent/): turn emails, flyers and screenshots into calendar events
    * [OmniSearch](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/omnisearch/): search any site from the address bar
    * Also [ConvertLocal](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/convertlocal/), [SpeakType](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/speaktype/), [ClearRec](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/clearrec/) and [FocusRead](https://wihoho.github.io/privacy/extensions/focusread/)
